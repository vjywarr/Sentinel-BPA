Research notes & limitations

Read before interpreting or presenting the figures

  • Population figures are gridded estimates from WorldPop and should not be described as official census counts.
  • Built-up area values are derived from the Dynamic World built-class workflow shown in the Earth Engine script.
  • The 68.71 km² net built-up increase and the 77.47 km² newly developed footprint are outputs from different analysis procedures; they are intentionally reported as separate indicators.
  • The 44.37% overlap is the reported proportion of the newly developed area intersecting the high-density zone mask.
  • Water demand is a scenario calculation based on 135 litres per person per day, not measured consumption.
  • Results depend on dataset resolution, classification threshold, boundary geometry and processing choices. Validate methods and dates before formal publication.
